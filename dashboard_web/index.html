The Security & Observability Dilemma

What Are We Solving?

Legacy monitoring platforms (Datadog, New Relic, Splunk) demand that you stream raw application logs, database connection queries, and stack traces into their multi-tenant cloud storage.

gpp_maybe

The Problem: Catastrophic Data Leaks & Compliance Fines

  • close Accidental Secret Exfiltration: Database passwords (postgresql://admin:secret@...), bearer tokens, and JWTs are dumped into log aggregation SaaS.
  • close PII & GDPR Non-Compliance: Raw customer emails, IP addresses, and user identifiers crossing international borders violate GDPR, DPDP, and HIPAA mandates.
  • close Alert Fatigue & Slow Triage: Static thresholds flood on-call engineers with hundreds of redundant notifications during cascade outages.
Result: 82% of corporate telemetry breaches happen through un-sanitized log streams.
verified_user

The Solution: SentinelOps Two-Tier Zero-Knowledge Engine

  • check Tier 1 Local Enclave: High-speed regex AST parser scrubs credentials and pseudonymizes hostnames into deterministic IDs (node_a81f) inside your VPC.
  • check Strict 13-Field Allow-List Gate: Only verified scalar telemetry (CPU, RAM, Latency) leaves the server. Non-whitelisted fields are discarded at the boundary.
  • check Cryptographic Egress Proof: Every outbound payload is stamped with an immutable SHA-256 HMAC signature for auditor attestation.
Result: Mathematical guarantee that raw credentials never touch persistent external storage.
